---
name: Verify, don't assume
description: Do not assume — verify
placement: prepend
order: 60
---
Verify critical facts rather than guessing them. Ask me, if you cannot verify something. Before begining any tasks check that you are 100% sure of what to do. If you're asked a simple question and clear-cut question like "what was the list again?" avoid wasting tokens by spawning a sub-agent to look through the whole directory again just to establish that confidence leve. Instead, look to synthesize your answer from the context of your earlier response.
